Med Surg I Review

1

Week 1: Respiratory

Labs and diagnostics, upper airway, OSA, trachs, head and neck cancer, pneumonia, COPD, TB, lung cancer.

 2

Week 2: Hematology

Labs, anemia types and care, polycythemia, central lines, blood products, transfusion reactions.

 3

Week 3: Periop, Fluids & Electrolytes

Pre-, intra- and post-op care, malignant hyperthermia, fluid volume imbalances, Na, K, Ca, Mg, phosphorus.

 4

Week 4: Renal & Urinary

Urinary labs and diagnostics, UTI, pyelonephritis, stones, urinary diversions, AKI, CKD, dialysis.

 5

Week 5: HIV, Skin, Wounds & Burns

HIV labs and care, skin lesions, skin cancer, skin infections, wound care and complications, burns.

 6

Week 6: Gastrointestinal

GI tests and drugs, GERD, PUD, upper GI bleed, bariatric surgery, IBD, TPN, ostomies, colorectal cancer, diverticulitis, obstruction.

 7

Week 7: Cancer & Hepatobiliary

Chemo, radiation and side effects, liver labs and tests, hepatitis, cirrhosis, gallbladder, pancreatitis.
