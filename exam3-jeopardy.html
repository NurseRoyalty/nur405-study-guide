Exam 3 Jeopardy

Built entirely from this site's content. Every question is NCLEX-NGN/clinical-judgment style with an answer and rationale -- nothing from outside this material. It covers Week 4 (endocrine labs and diagnostics, ADH disorders, adrenal disorders, thyroid disorders, diabetes) and Week 5 (fractures, immobilization devices and amputations; joint surgeries, osteomyelitis, herniated discs and osteoporosis; osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, gout and lupus).

A 3-player game: each round randomly draws 6 categories, 3 from Week 4 and 3 from Week 5 (30 questions total), with difficulty rising from 100 to 500 points within every category. Click a value, read the question, then hit Reveal Answer -- the answer and rationale won't show until then. Award or dock points to Player 1, 2, or 3 as your group agrees on who got it. "New Game" draws new categories and new questions every time; "Reset Game" replays the current board from scratch.
