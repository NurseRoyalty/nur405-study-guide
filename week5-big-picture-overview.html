High-Alert Safety Rules — All in One Place

The danger callouts and safety statements from across the three pages, condensed. Click through to a topic for the full explanation.

TopicThe Hard Rules
Fractures, Devices & AmputationsAirway trumps a healing mandible (pliers at home). Pain unrelieved by medication is the first sign: notify the provider. Neuro checks every hour for 24 hours after a cast. Compartment syndrome is an emergency (fasciotomy). Traction weights hang free and the line of pull is never interrupted. No ointments on pins; nothing inside a cast. Watch for hemorrhage under the residual limb; prevent hip flexion contracture.
Joint Surgeries, Osteomyelitis, Discs & OsteoporosisNo metal in an MRI. Lie flat after a myelogram. VTE is the number one joint replacement complication. Posterior hip: no internal rotation, adduction or flexion past 90°; anterior hip: no hyperextension. Only the surgeon removes the original dressing. Log roll after back surgery; clear light-yellow drainage plus headache is a CSF leak. Alendronate: water, empty stomach, upright 30 minutes.
OA, RA, Gout & SLEAcetaminophen limit 3 g a day (4 g only short term and supervised). No more than 3 or 4 steroid joint injections a year; no systemic steroids for OA. Start RA DMARDs early. Steroids: lowest dose, shortest time. Gout: keep the sheets off, encourage fluids. SLE: avoid sunlight and infection, don't stop drugs abruptly, monitor BUN, creatinine, urine output and neuro status.
